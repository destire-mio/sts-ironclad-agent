# E128 training recovery after output-pipe failure

On September 22, the main task resumed the unstarted E128 training recipe using the completed E127 data. Collection had finished 17,552 relic and 69,599 joint continuations, including independent audits of 4,388 and 4,387 families. The joint audit verified 9,981,559 outside neural choices and recorded zero faults. These are continuation labels, not policy wins.

The collection child exited 0 and its completion proofs were written before the controller failed at `run_pipeline.py:130`. Printing the completed result to the interactive output pipe raised `BrokenPipeError`; execution never reached training. The exception proves the output consumer had closed, but does not identify why the consumer closed. The historical failed controller record remains intact.

The recovery entry in `agent/heart_training_resume.py` launches a detached controller with a regular file for stdout/stderr and `/dev/null` for stdin. It enters the original training entry after checking the completed collection, unchanged registrations and full source/label admission. Training still owns the original stage deadlines and worker cleanup. Exclusive execution directories and logs reject duplicate starts. The recipe, frozen model/engine, family roles and collection files are unchanged; no collection or fitting attempt was rerun.

Three focused regression checks pass. With the same small worker, closing the pipe prevents the next stage in the old path; the file-backed controller completes after its launcher exits and retains stdout/stderr. A failing worker preserves its nonzero exit and log, and an existing execution is rejected. The real entry passed full frozen admission. Live process inspection then verified the training controller, fit child, `/dev/null` stdin and both output descriptors pointing to the same regular log file. Both subsequent fits completed 1,000 updates. Their checkpoint hashes match the freeze record, and the fit stage exited 0 with verified cleanup. Live-choice validation remains pending; this is not a completed development study or a win-rate gain.

The two arms use 1,536 and 4,608 fit families with the same 1,024 label-holdout families. Both checkpoints are frozen before holdout scoring. Each eligible arm then runs the registered 512-family natural-development screen. All original gates and the independent unseen acceptance requirement remain.
